AUTO SALE WORKFLOW

This module automates your entire sales process, from confirming quotations to creating sales orders and managing invoices.
It reduces manual work, minimizes errors, and ensures every step is completed efficiently.
Odoo Sale Auto Workflow helps you save time, improve accuracy, and maintain a smooth and organized sales process.

Features

Automatically confirm and validate sales orders with one click.

Choose automatic invoice creation based on your preferences.

Automatically register payments for completed invoices.

Select shipping and invoicing policies for specific orders.

Enable or disable features per order or globally in settings.

Configuration

Configuration per sales order

Manage invoice policies individually per sales order

Configure payment journal per sales order

Confirm the Order to Trigger Automation

Automatically creates delivery and invoice on order confirmation based on configured workflow settings
